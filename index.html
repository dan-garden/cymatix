      Calibrate from measurements
      
A real plate rarely matches its datasheet. Enter resonances you measured, and the fit finds the frequency scale and, on a shaker, the moving mass. It keeps these settings for shape, edges and driver position.

      
      

      

    

  


  

    Driver
    
    
    
    
    
    
Point force: a free plate pushed at the marker. Bolted to shaker: a real Chladni rig, where the shaker's moving mass (armature, screw, washer) is fixed to the plate at the marker. Rigid bolt: that mass is infinite, so the marker moves exactly with the drive. Click the plate to move the marker.

    Sand
    
    
    
    
    
Grains move at their real calculated speed, which can take minutes. Time-lapse speeds it up, and ×1 is real time. Fine powder only moves to antinodes under strong drive, and the direction it takes is the least certain part of the model.
